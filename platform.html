Why the platform matters

Every module shares the same identity, workflow, data, and intelligence layer.

What it is

The operating layer under every FoundrySuite product. Identity, workflows, integrations, documents, analytics, and AI are shared services — not extras you stitch on later.

Who it serves

Growing companies and enterprises rolling out manufacturing, CRM, projects, people, finance, and industry solutions — without rebuilding the same foundations for each domain.

How it works

You choose the modules you need. They sit on the same 19 engines, so roles, processes, and connectors are configured once — and industry solutions inherit the same upgrade path.
